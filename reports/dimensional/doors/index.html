One room, four doorways: projection study

The editor renderer remains available. The candidate is an isolated experiment with a 32 × 24 visual floor cell, a shared wall-height vector, and geometry-owned door frames. Logical cells remain square and unchanged. This is a visual study, not an approved replacement tileset.

Door details in the same fixed viewing direction

North and south occupy horizontal walls; east and west occupy vertical walls. The latter are profiles, rather than quarter-turned frontal arches. A fixed camera sees the same south/east masonry surfaces on opposite room boundaries.
