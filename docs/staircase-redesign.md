# Architectural staircase replacement — Three.js milestone

The experimental Three.js renderer now uses a new masonry staircase module instead of the old six-step, single-tile prop. The old staircase-building loop, its 0.72-unit rise, and its tiny descending aperture have been removed from `scene.ts`. The production Canvas renderer remains unchanged.

`stone-staircase.ts` builds two opposing flights with closed risers, substantial beveled tread slabs, a full-width return landing, bonded stone foundations, retaining cheeks and an arrival terrace. The vertical slab edges have their own darker, textured stone material. Their lighter upper faces and bevels make each tread distinguishable from the foundation without relying on cast shadows. Foundation courses use staggered joints. All stone surfaces use the existing Ancient masonry atlas and seeded wear; approved caps are untouched.

The ascent starts at the room paving height, 0.033 units, and ends at wall height plus the approved 0.085-unit coping height. Step count grows with wall height, keeping risers at or below 0.18 units. Where an adjoining wall is available, the upper terrace joins its crest through a short stone extension. A separate upper floor beyond that terrace is not generated by this prototype.

The descending version cuts a full stair bay out of the paving. Two flights descend through a stone-lined well to a lower landing at minus wall height. Narrow room arms use a matching straight flight with the same slab construction and complete rise/descent. Very confined bays consequently have narrower or steeper flights than the standard switchback.

`stair-layout.ts` fits each presentation bay into actual floor cells in the same room, clears door openings and other stair bays, and prefers an adjacent wall for the upper endpoint. It can shift the displayed structure within its room to fit real architecture. Core stair positions, generator output, traversal and collision data are unchanged. The inspection selector now focuses on the displayed bay and its full height.

## Validation

- `npm test`: 154 passing tests. New coverage fits 80 generated 32/64 maps, checks generation immutability, deterministic placement and door clearance, and ray-tests every tread and destination landing in both forms/directions at wall heights 2, 2.5 and 3.5. It also checks finite geometry, complete floor apertures and that dark edge faces partition the slab without duplicate overlapping surfaces.
- App TypeScript, ESLint and production build pass.
- `node --experimental-strip-types scripts/verify-staircase-redesign.mjs` captures all views, ray-checks actual scene treads/landings, verifies the highest wall setting and retained floor geometry, and compares approved cap fingerprints to the preceding runtime-fix report when available. This run matched the baseline and produced no browser errors.
- The existing `verify-dungeon-3d.mjs` regression suite passes. Its stair probes now inspect the fitted presentation bay rather than assuming the old one-cell model's position. Its generation, portal, camera, secret-concealment, resource and Canvas-parity assertions remain in place.

Browser verification uses the shared headless helper and its temporary workspace profile on D:. Close each session before starting the next one.
